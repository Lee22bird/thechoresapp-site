Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

The short version

The Chores helps families track chores and allowances. We collect the minimum needed to make that work, we never sell your data, we show no ads, and no real money moves through the app — it only keeps a record of what a parent owes and when they paid it.

Why there is a small monthly fee

We chose a subscription model so The Chores can be supported by the families who use it, rather than by selling attention or private household data. The fee helps cover hosting, support, security, and ongoing development. We would rather build a useful app that lasts than become a business built around monetizing your family's information.

What we collect

  • Parent accounts. An email address and password, used to sign in. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are never visible to us.
  • Household details. Your household name and the first names or nicknames, optional ages, and sign-in PINs of the members a parent adds.
  • Chore and allowance records. The chores you create, completions, balances, and cash-out history.

We do not collect payment information, location, contacts, or advertising identifiers.

Children's privacy

Child profiles are created and controlled entirely by a parent. Children do not register themselves, have no email accounts with us, and sign in only with a PIN inside their parent's household. The only information about a child we hold is what their parent chooses to enter. Parents can edit or delete a child's profile — and everything recorded under it — at any time.

Where your data lives

Data is stored with our database provider, Supabase, on servers in the United States, and is encrypted in transit. Access is restricted by per-household security rules: members of one household can never see another household's data, and children can only see their own lists.

What we never do

  • Sell or rent your data to anyone
  • Show advertising of any kind
  • Use third-party trackers or analytics that identify your family

Deleting your data

Deleting your household in the app removes its records. You can also email us (see Support) and we will delete your account and all associated data.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
