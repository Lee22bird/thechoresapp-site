Common questions

Does the app move real money? No. It records what a parent owes and when it was paid. The handing-over of actual cash happens in real life, between you.

Can my child see other people's chores? Children see only their own list. Parents see the whole household.

How do I delete our data? Delete the household in the app, or email us and we'll remove everything. See our Privacy Policy.
